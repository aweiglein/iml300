Project 1: i blinked

After getting to college and being away from my family, time really started to feel finite. When we're away from our usual routine, the place we grew up, our comfy bed at home with our stuffed animals, there's a shift in time that we can really feel. Even if we go back home we know that we're leaving again eventually. Adults in our lives like to say "I blinked and you grew up", and we probably shrugged and went on with our days, but now those words are hitting me stronger than ever before. This project demonstrates how fast time is now starting to pass by. I wanted to use one of my stuffed bears from home to represent the college kid that still is holding on to a part of their childhood as they move through the world. As the user clicks the screen, the background changes seasons and matching nostalgic music plays in the background.

Project 2: for noni

Play Project 2: for noni

I’ve always heard people say “death isn’t easy” growing up, but I never really thought much about it until recently. My grandma was a burst of sparkly purple laughter. She'd always correct your grammar but wouldn't miss a beat to stand up for you through thick and thin. She loved matzo ball soup, as she grew up Jewish, watched Say Yes to the Dress on TV, and hung up every picture I’d ever drawn for her on her kitchen walls. At our age, she was a spunky daredevil who rode motorcycles, climbed through windows to watch the Beatles on TV in college, and could make any costume you could dream up. But the most important thing was that she loved. Hard. And she fought to be with the people she loved. She beat cancer four times, developed kidney failure, and began spending her days watching Say Yes to the Dress in the hospital. But nonetheless, she fought to stay alive and be with my family. After a while, it was time for her to rest. This project is an ode to her. I love you, Noni.
